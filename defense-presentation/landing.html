Paper Records Are Holding Your School Back

Schools across the region still rely on handwritten ledgers. Here's what that really costs — and how SchoolPulse fixes it.

Lost or Damaged Records

Floods, fires, or simple misplacement can erase years of student data with no backup.

Manual Rank Calculation

Teachers spend hours hand-calculating averages, ranks, and semester grades — prone to errors.

Internet Dependency

Most digital tools stop working the moment connectivity drops — a daily reality in rural areas.

No Parent Communication

Parents rarely see their child's progress until report card day — too late to intervene.

Secure, Backed-Up Storage

All student data stored securely with automatic backups — no more lost records, ever.

Auto-Calculated Ranks & Averages

Enter scores once — ranks, semester grades, and radar charts generate instantly.

Offline-First Architecture

Keep entering data without internet. It queues locally and syncs automatically when connected.

Direct Parent Messaging

Parents view reports via a simple link and exchange text or voice messages with teachers.

Role-Based Dashboards

Admin, Teacher, Librarian, and Super Admin each see a tailored workspace with relevant tools and data — nothing more, nothing less.

Score Entry & Auto-Ranking

Enter monthly and semester scores with automatic rank calculation, radar-chart performance summaries, and instant grade overviews per student.

Offline-First Data Entry

Parent Portal (No Login)

Offline-First

Designed for
Real-World Connectivity

Many schools face unreliable or nonexistent internet. Our offline queue captures every entry locally on the device, then syncs seamlessly when you're back online. No data lost. No extra steps. No stress.

Automatic Queue & Sync
Data saves locally first, uploads when connection is detected — fully hands-free.
Conflict Resolution Built In
If two teachers edit the same record offline, the system merges intelligently — no overwritten data.
PWA — Installable as a Native App
