What We Offer

Everything Your School Needs

Six powerful modules designed to handle every aspect of school administration — from student enrollment to parent communication.

Student Management

Register, track, and manage student profiles, health records, and growth charts — all in one place.

Score & Ranking

Monthly & semester score entry with auto-ranking and PDF report cards ready to print or share.

Attendance Tracking

Daily attendance marking with real-time stats and auto-calculated attendance rates per student.

Library Management

Book catalog, borrow/return tracking, and automatic overdue alerts to keep your library organized.

Budget & Inventory

Track school finances and inventory with running balance, expense categories, and audit trails.

Parent Reports

Share report cards via link — no login needed. Includes principal approval workflow for quality control.
